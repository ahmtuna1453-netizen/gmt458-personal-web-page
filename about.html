About me

I am a fourth-year Geomatics Engineering student at Hacettepe University. During my studies, I have worked with geographic information and mapping tools, including Python, QGIS, and Netcad.

This website is a practical learning project where I am exploring how interactive maps can be presented on the web using OpenLayers and Leaflet. The locations shown on the maps are examples for demonstrating the map features.
